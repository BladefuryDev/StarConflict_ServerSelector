# Changelog

## v1.2.2 — Bug fixes

**Login fields (UI Overhaul)**
- Clicking into the E-mail or Password field selects its text again, and leaving a field deselects it. Only the field you're typing in is highlighted.
- The text cursor now sits right after the last typed character instead of overlapping it.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move between the login fields, including the server address and port fields when they're shown. Tab keeps working after you click elsewhere on the screen.
- Ticking "Remember me" before filling in the form no longer folds the form away while you're still moving between fields.

**Golden Play button (UI Overhaul)**
- The "Play" label no longer appears before the button itself during the intro; it now fades in with the button.
- Hovering the Play button makes it grow evenly from its centre and lift straight up, instead of drifting to the right.

To update, download the new patcher and click **↻ Reinstall / Update** on the UI Overhaul tab. The Server Selector is unchanged in this release.
